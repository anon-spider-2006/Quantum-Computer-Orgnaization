# Quantum-Computer-Orgnaization

This repository contains my projects and assignments from Quantum Computer Organization at RPI.
The coursework focuses on the practical and theoretical foundations of quantum computing, with an emphasis on implementing, analyzing, and running quantum circuits using Qiskit and IBM Quantum systems.

## Topics
The repository covers topics including:

Quantum circuit design and simulation
Qiskit programming and circuit construction
Quantum algorithms, including QAOA and VQE
Circuit transpilation, synthesis, and optimization
Qubit connectivity and hardware-aware circuit mapping
Quantum noise and error characterization
Error mitigation and suppression
Quantum state preparation and measurement
Hamiltonian simulation
Variational and sampling-based quantum algorithms
Fault-tolerant quantum computing
Experiments on IBM Quantum hardware
